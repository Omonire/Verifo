Every document gets four independent checks

Each check scores 0–100. A weighted blend produces one confidence score with a clear verdict — and the reasons are always explained.

01

Readability (OCR)

Is the document cleanly readable? Blurry scans and poor quality pull this score down.

02

Template match

Does layout, typography and seal line up with the trusted reference original?

03

Registry match

Do the issuing office's own records agree? Full agreement scores 100.

04

Tamper signals

A forensic scan for edits — metadata rewrites, cloned pixels, shifted seals.
